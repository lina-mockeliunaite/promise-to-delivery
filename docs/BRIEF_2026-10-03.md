## Asymmetry rule (applies to every step)

## Sign-off clarifications (Lina, 2 Oct, 18:02) — binding, within the planned steps

1. **Incomplete keys stay separate.** Two statements never merge merely because both have missing terms. Required terms are defined per capability or promise type; for a volume, that includes the unit and the period.
2. **Multiple term sets preserve explicit pairings.** Expand shared networks or assets only where the wording supports those combinations. No Cartesian product when particular assets are paired with particular networks; an ambiguous association gets `terms_incomplete`.
3. **Link eligibility is enforced in the database.** Foreign keys and triggers preserve the statement / review / source-version identity and the `kept = 1` requirement, including preventing an already-linked statement from being changed to dropped.
4. **Scoring exposes under-merging.** Predicted groups are matched to labelled commitments one-to-one; unmatched groups count as mismatches, so fragmented groups cannot all map to one label and inflate the score. Labels never influence how groups are built.

Order, four-hour ceiling and cut rules are unchanged.
