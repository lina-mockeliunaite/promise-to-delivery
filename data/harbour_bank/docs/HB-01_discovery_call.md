# Discovery call — Harbour Bank / Elva

Date: 28 September 2026

Attendees: Daniel Ong (Account Executive, Elva), Priya Raman (Solutions Engineer, Elva), Aisha Rahman (Head of AML Operations, Harbour Bank), Marcus Lim (Head of Technology, Harbour Bank)

**Aisha Rahman:** Our conventional payment operations already use Elva's transaction-monitoring and case-management workflow. We're launching HarbourPay Global on 1 December. SMEs will pay suppliers in the Philippines and Vietnam in NUSD, a USD stablecoin. Most payouts will use Polygon; larger ones will use Ethereum. We expect about 8,000 payouts a day at launch and are planning for up to 40,000 by the end of year one. We've announced the launch date to our partner merchants.

**Aisha Rahman:** Our launch design is to hold each payout until screening clears the recipient wallet.

**Marcus Lim:** Our payout engine will call Elva's screening API and hold the payout until it gets a clear result back.

**Priya Raman:** That's a good fit for how we work. We're exploring whether Elva's pre-built connector to a blockchain analytics provider can return real-time screening results for Polygon wallets in time for your launch. I'll bring our Product team to the next call so you hear it first-hand.

**Marcus Lim:** Please do. What do you need from us to get started?

**Priya Raman:** Mainly environment access. We're targeting a 1 December go-live, provided Harbour Bank's sandbox and testnet access is available by 15 November.

**Marcus Lim:** We can provide sandbox and testnet access by 15 November.

**Daniel Ong:** Great. We'll respond to your RFP within the window and follow with a proposal.
