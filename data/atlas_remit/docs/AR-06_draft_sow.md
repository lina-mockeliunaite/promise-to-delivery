# STATEMENT OF WORK No. 1 — DRAFT v0.3

**Atlas Direct implementation**

Date: 26 October 2026
Parties: Elva Pte. Ltd. ("Elva") and Atlas Remit Pte. Ltd. ("Atlas")

This Statement of Work is issued under the Master Subscription and Services Agreement between the parties. Annex 1 (Wallet Screening) forms part of this Statement of Work. Draft for discussion only; subject to contract.

## 1. Background

Atlas is launching Atlas Direct, an NUSD payout service, targeted for 1 February 2027. This SOW sets out the Elva services for that launch.

## 2. Platform

2.1 Elva will deploy the Atlas tenant on Amazon Web Services in the Singapore region.

2.2 Elva will configure the case management workspace, including case audit history, for the Atlas investigations team.

## 3. Name and sanctions screening

3.1 Elva will screen customer and beneficiary names against sanctions lists at volumes of up to 30,000 screenings per day.

## 4. Payout ledger integration

4.1 The payout ledger connector will support up to thirty thousand (30,000) payouts per day.

## 5. Wallet screening

5.1 Elva will provide NUSD wallet screening as set out in Annex 1.

## 6. Services and support

6.1 Elva will provide 30 days of hypercare support following go-live.

6.2 Atlas will provide sandbox access and analytics-provider credentials as reasonably requested by Elva.

## 7. Project management

7.1 Change requests will follow the procedure in clause 11 of the Agreement.

---

## Annex 1 — Wallet Screening

1.1 Ethereum: each beneficiary wallet is screened in real time through the analytics integration before the NUSD payout is released.

1.2 Polygon: each beneficiary wallet is screened in real time through the analytics integration before the NUSD payout is released, under the limited-beta support terms approved for Atlas.

1.3 Atlas supplies and maintains its own licence and API credentials with its blockchain analytics provider.
