# Response to Alder Bank RFP ALD-SCAM-2026-03 — Instant Payment Scam Protection

Date: 29 September 2026

Submitted by Elva

**Section 2.1 — Behavioural signals**

*Question 2.1.1: Describe how you detect customers who are being coached by a scammer.*

Through its connector to BioCatch, Elva will use behavioural signals from Alder's mobile and internet banking to flag customers who appear to be coached by a scammer, live from the 1 February 2027 launch.

*Question 4.2.1: Where will the service be hosted?*

Elva will host Alder's dedicated environment on Microsoft Azure in the Australia East region.

*Question 6.1.1: Describe your implementation methodology.*

Elva follows a phased methodology: discovery, configuration, testing and launch readiness. A detailed plan will follow in our proposal.
