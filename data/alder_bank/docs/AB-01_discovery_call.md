# Discovery call — Alder Bank / Elva

Date: 15 September 2026

Attendees: Tom Fraser (Account Executive, Elva), Mei Chen (Solutions Engineer, Elva), Rachel O'Neill (Head of Fraud and Scams, Alder Bank), Sanjay Patel (Head of Payments Technology, Alder Bank)

**Rachel O'Neill:** Our customers are losing money to scams on instant payments. Someone gets a call from a fake "bank fraud team", they're talked into sending money by PayID, and it arrives in seconds. Once it's gone, it's gone. We want to stop the payment before it leaves, not chase it afterwards.

**Rachel O'Neill:** We're launching scam protection on all outbound instant payments on 1 February 2027. We've told our board that date.

**Sanjay Patel:** Today we send about 180,000 instant payments a day. With the new mobile app we're planning for 750,000 a day by the end of the first year. Our payments platform would hold each payment until Elva returns a decision.

**Mei Chen:** That's exactly the pattern we support. We're also exploring whether we could add a payee name check next year; I'll bring our Product lead to the technical call so you hear it first-hand.

**Sanjay Patel:** Good. We run everything on Microsoft Azure today, so hosting matters to us.

**Tom Fraser:** Understood. We'll respond to your RFP within the window and follow with a proposal.
