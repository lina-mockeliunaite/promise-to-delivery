# Proposal — Instant Payment Scam Protection for Alder Bank

Date: 2 October 2026

Prepared by Elva for Alder Bank

## 1. Summary

This proposal describes Elva's scam protection for Alder Bank's outbound instant payments from the 1 February 2027 launch and through the first year of operation.

## 2. Real-time payment decisions

All outbound instant payments will be scored in real time before they are released.

Real-time scoring will handle up to 200,000 payments per day at launch.

## 3. Scam-call alerts

Scam-call alerts will warn the bank when a customer seems to be on a call with a scammer while making a payment, from the 1 February 2027 launch.

## 4. Investigations

Payments held for review will open as cases in Elva's case management workspace, with a full case audit history.

Commercial terms are set out in the accompanying pricing schedule. A draft Statement of Work will follow.
