# Discovery call — Noma Journeys / Aivuna

Date: 14 September 2026

Attendees: Liam Hart (Account Executive, Aivuna), Yuki Tan (Solutions Engineer, Aivuna), Celeste Wong (Head of Guest Experience, Noma Journeys), Ravi Menon (Head of Operations Technology, Noma Journeys)

**Celeste Wong:** We arrange tailored holidays across Asia for travellers from all over the world. Guests write to us before they travel, and again while they are on the road, to change a booking or ask about a refund. Our team is stretched, and guests wait too long for an answer.

**Celeste Wong:** We want an assistant that answers those questions for us, and we want to launch it on 1 February 2027. We have already told our leadership that date.

**Ravi Menon:** Our bookings live in our own booking system. The assistant would need to read a guest's booking before it answers, and pass anything it can't resolve to our agents.

**Yuki Tan:** That's the pattern we support. We're also exploring whether we could add a WhatsApp channel next year; I'll bring our Product lead to the demo so you hear it first-hand.

**Celeste Wong:** Good. Support during the launch itself worries me. This is our first assistant.

**Liam Hart:** A dedicated Aivuna launch specialist will work with your team for the first 30 days after launch, at no extra charge.

**Celeste Wong:** That would help a great deal.

**Liam Hart:** We'll respond to your RFP within the window and follow with a proposal.
