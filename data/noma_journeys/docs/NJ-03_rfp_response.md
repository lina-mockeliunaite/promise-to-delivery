# Response to Noma Journeys RFP NJ-CS-2026-02 — AI Customer Support Assistant

Date: 28 September 2026

Submitted by Aivuna

**Section 2.1 — Languages**

*Question 2.1.1: Which languages will the assistant support at launch?*

Aivuna will support guest conversations in 12 languages from the 1 February 2027 launch.

**Section 3.1 — Channels**

*Question 3.1.1: Through which channels will guests reach the assistant?*

Aivuna's assistant will answer guests' phone calls and respond to chat and email from the 1 February 2027 launch.

**Section 5.1 — Launch support**

*Question 5.1.1: What support will Noma Journeys receive during launch?*

A dedicated Aivuna launch specialist will work with Noma Journeys for the first 30 days after launch at no extra charge.

**Section 6.1 — Implementation approach**

*Question 6.1.1: Describe your implementation methodology.*

Aivuna follows a phased methodology: discovery, configuration, testing and launch readiness. A detailed plan will follow in our proposal.
