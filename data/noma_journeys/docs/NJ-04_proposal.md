# Proposal — AI Customer Support Assistant for Noma Journeys

Date: 5 October 2026

Prepared by Aivuna for Noma Journeys

## 1. Summary

This proposal describes Aivuna's AI customer-support assistant for Noma Journeys' guests from the 1 February 2027 launch.

## 2. Languages

Aivuna will support guest conversations in 12 languages from the 1 February 2027 launch, including English, Mandarin Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

## 3. Channels

The assistant will respond to guests by chat and email from the 1 February 2027 launch.

## 4. Bookings, changes and refunds

The assistant will read each guest's booking from Noma's booking system to answer questions about bookings, changes and refunds.

Conversations the assistant cannot resolve will be handed to Noma's human agents with the full conversation attached.

## 5. Launch support

A dedicated Aivuna launch specialist will work with Noma Journeys for the first 30 days after launch at no extra charge.
