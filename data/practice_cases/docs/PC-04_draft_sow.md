# DRAFT Statement of Work — Tidewater Pay

Date: 16 November 2026

Between Elva ("Provider") and Tidewater Pay ("Customer"). Draft — subject to contract.

## 1. Wallet screening

Ethereum: each recipient wallet is screened in real time before the payout is released.

Polygon: recipient wallets are screened in hourly batches before release.

## 2. Investigations

Case audit history is provided for every case.

Alert assignment and SLA tracking is configured for the operations team.

## 3. Volumes

The payout ledger connector will handle up to 20,000 payouts per day.
