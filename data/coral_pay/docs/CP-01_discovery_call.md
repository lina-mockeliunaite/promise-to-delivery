# Discovery call — Coral Pay / Elva

Date: 2 November 2026

Attendees: Sarah Whitfield (Account Executive, Elva), Ben Carter (Solutions Engineer, Elva), Olivia Tran (Head of Compliance Operations, Coral Pay), Raj Patel (Chief Technology Officer, Coral Pay)

**Olivia Tran:** We're launching contractor payouts across Asia on 15 January. Clients fund in AUD, and contractors are paid in NUSD, on Ethereum or Solana depending on where their wallet sits. We expect around 12,000 payouts a day at launch.

**Olivia Tran:** Ethereum is covered. We already screen our Ethereum payout wallets through our own provider, and that stays as it is. What we're evaluating Elva for is the Solana route and the wider workflow: sanctions screening of payer and beneficiary names, case management for payout exceptions, and a connection to our payout ledger.

**Olivia Tran:** Our launch policy is simple. We will hold each contractor payout until its recipient-wallet screening decision is clear.

**Ben Carter:** That makes sense, and the wider workflow is core Elva. We're exploring whether Elva's integration could screen NUSD recipient wallets on Solana for your Australian launch. I'll bring our implementation lead to the next call so we can talk through the path to go-live.

**Raj Patel:** Good. One thing to flag early: our infrastructure standard is Azure. Our security team will send your team a deployment questionnaire this week.

**Sarah Whitfield:** We'll turn that around quickly and follow with a proposal.
