# Proposal — Compliance controls for Coral Pay contractor payouts

Date: 16 November 2026

Prepared by Elva for Coral Pay

## 1. Summary

This proposal sets out Elva's support for Coral Pay's contractor payout launch on 15 January 2027.

## 2. Wallet screening

Elva will screen the recipient wallet of every NUSD payout on Solana through its pre-built analytics integration before release.

Coral Pay's Ethereum payouts will continue to be screened through Coral Pay's own provider.

## 3. Payout ledger integration

The payout ledger connector will handle up to 12,000 payouts per day at launch.

## 4. Sanctions screening

Elva's sanctions screening API will screen up to 25,000 payer and beneficiary names per day.

## 5. Payout exceptions

Payout exceptions will be managed in Elva's case management workspace.

## 6. Launch readiness

Elva will deliver two 90-minute operations training sessions for Coral Pay's payout operations team before launch.

## 7. Next steps

A draft Statement of Work will follow.
